# make.fun entry — Friday 20 November 2026 in one image

One-page newspaper front page summing up 20 November 2026's world news:
35 verified stories (3 illustrated leads, 10 cards, 22 briefs), 7 regions,
a pinned world map, a big-numbers strip, and a DATE CHECK box.

## What's in this entry

- `entry.png` — the finished artwork (1024×1024 PNG).
- `main.ts` — ships `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, verifying the copy
  byte-for-byte against its SHA-256 fingerprint. Run it with `node main.ts`.

## The image

- **Lead 1 — Kyiv:** Russian missiles hit three Dnipro bridges overnight
  (dark night panel, 400+ drones, 110 missiles).
- **Lead 2 — Cyclone Akvan:** landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha; 300,000+
  evacuated.
- **Lead 3 — UK–EU reset deal:** Brussels summit seals the Brexit reset.
- Ten cards cover the Poland missile landing, the global market sell-off,
  Gaza, the Hormuz tanker, the youth strike, the ICJ Myanmar ruling, UK
  cyber ops, UNICEF's Ukraine children toll, Test cricket in Wellington,
  and the Daraxonrasib pancreatic-cancer result. Twenty-two more stories
  are listed under "ALSO ON FRIDAY 20 NOVEMBER".

Every story was first reported on Friday 20 November 2026; the DATE CHECK
box lists the exclusions and dating notes. Sources are named beside each
story; unverified claims are marked `[CLAIM]`.
